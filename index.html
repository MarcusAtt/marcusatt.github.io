About Me

I am an emerging public relations and strategic communication professional pursuing my Bachelor of Arts in Communication with an emphasis in Public Relations at California State Polytechnic University, Pomona. My academic background centers on media relations, audience research, campaign design, and visual communication ethics.

Beyond the classroom, I serve as the co-creator and brand strategist for ThePopTwins, a creator brand across Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube focused on pop culture entertainment, rare collectibles, and live social commerce. In this role, I manage end-to-end digital operations, including editorial planning, audience sentiment analysis, short-form video production, and community moderation. By integrating core PR principles—such as message framing, authentic audience engagement, and ethical disclosure—I help translate organic video engagement into loyal, high-retention community followers and verified auction sales.

My professional toolset includes certified expertise in graphic design and visual communications (Canva Essentials, Graphic Design Principles), video editing for multi-platform distribution, and comprehensive campaign scheduling via Meta Business Suite. Complementing my creative and strategic communications work, my background in operational and customer-facing roles at the YMCA has honed my adaptability, conflict resolution, and stakeholder communication in fast-paced environments.

Driven by a passion for the evolving entertainment landscape, my career goal is to join an entertainment PR or digital publicity team at major media organizations, such as NBCUniversal or Warner Bros. Discovery. I aim to contribute to high-impact theatrical releases, streaming publicity campaigns, and creator partnership strategies that bridge traditional media relations with digital audience fandoms.
